03 · Locked

Hero direction — dark wins

Locked: dark hero. Espresso bg, paper headline, honey emphasis, burnt-orange CTA — schedule strip moves to --espresso-soft so honey numbers stay the only signal. Light alternative kept for reference.

04 · Rhythm

One dark block per page — subscription

Espresso section breaks the cream scroll and gives honey its only stage. Prices stay honey on dark; on light sections prices are accent — never both on the same surface.
